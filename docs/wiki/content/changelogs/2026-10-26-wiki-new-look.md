# Changelog - 2026-10-26

## The wiki has a new look

<Badge type="tip" text="Enhancement" />

**In one line.** The IGNIS wiki now looks like the other Venizia wikis, with a new home page, header and footer. Only readers are affected.

## What changed

- **Header.** A grid button opens the Venizia ecosystem: Venizia, IGNIS, ARDOR and VULCAN, with "You are here" on this one. The light and dark switch reveals the new theme from where you click.
- **Doc pages.** New type (Inter Display and JetBrains Mono, served from this site, not from Google), sharp corners and hairline rules. Code windows are dark in both themes. The sidebar and outline mark the current item with a tint, not a side bar.
- **Home page.** The headline is "Focus on business value. IGNIS holds the discipline." Every number on it is counted from the repository, and a test fails when one drifts. The authentication card says what is not finished: Casbin authorization is experimental and OAuth2 is not implemented.
- **Footer.** One footer on every page: this wiki's links, the ecosystem, the community and the company.

## Who is affected

- **You read the wiki.** Nothing to do. Bookmarks and links keep working: no page moved.
- **You build on IGNIS.** No package changed.
